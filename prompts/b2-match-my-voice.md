# B2 · Teach it your voice.

**Bonus · use it on any answer** · About 5 min the first time

> **Use it when:** You want every draft to sound like you wrote it.  
> **Have ready:** 2 or 3 emails or posts you wrote

Give it a few things you actually wrote: emails, posts, texts. It picks up your words, your rhythm and your personality, then adapts.

## Copy this

```text
Below are a few things I wrote myself. Study how I write: word choice, sentence length, how formal I am, how I open and sign off.

Then rewrite my draft so it sounds like me. Match my personality, not just my grammar. Do not copy lines from my samples.

HOW I'D DESCRIBE MY STYLE: [3 words, like direct, warm, a bit funny]
MY SAMPLES: [paste or upload 2 or 3 emails or posts]
DRAFT: [paste it]
```

Swap every `[bracketed]` part for your own details.

## Made-up example

**You paste:** Three of your own emails, the words “direct, warm, a bit funny” and a stiff intro email.

**What changes:**

Shorter sentences, your usual sign-off and the kind of line you would actually say. Same facts.

## Then do this

- [ ] Read it out loud
- [ ] Fix any line you would never say
- [ ] Save your samples in one note for next time

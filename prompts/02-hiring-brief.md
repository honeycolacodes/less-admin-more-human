# 02 · Before you hire, get clear on the work.

**For founders + hiring managers** · About 5 min

> **Use it when:** The brief now lists everything the team might ever need.  
> **Have ready:** Your rough hiring notes, messy is fine

## Copy this

```text
Turn these rough hiring notes into a short brief for a sourcer.

Sort it into:
1. What this person must deliver in their first 6 months.
2. Skills that are essential to those outcomes, with a reason for each.
3. Skills they could learn on the job.
4. Three decisions we need to make before the search starts.

Treat your suggestions as proposals, not agreed requirements. Flag anything that contradicts itself. Do not add requirements just because similar job ads list them.

HIRING NOTES: [paste them here]
```

Swap every `[bracketed]` part for your own details.

## Made-up example

**You paste:** “We need a Product Manager for our GenAI assistant. Technical enough to write prompts and read eval results. Pricing experience would be nice. Not sure yet if they report to product or engineering.”

**A decision it should surface:**

“Does this PM report to product or engineering? That changes who you are looking for.”

## Then do this

- [ ] Settle the 3 open decisions with your team
- [ ] Cut anything nobody can defend
- [ ] Send the shorter brief. Clearer search, clearer pitch.

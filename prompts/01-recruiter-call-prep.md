# 01 · Walk in with better questions.

**For job seekers** · About 5 min

> **Use it when:** A recruiter call is coming up and you have way too many tabs open.  
> **Have ready:** The job post + 5 facts about your work

## Copy this

```text
Help me prep for a recruiter call. Use only what I paste below.

Give me:
1. Five specific questions about the role, the team and how the work actually runs.
2. Three moments from my background worth bringing up, and why each one fits.
3. Anything important the job post leaves unclear.

Do not invent experience or script my answers. Keep it to one page.

JOB POST: [paste it here]
MY BACKGROUND: [5 factual bullet points]
```

Swap every `[bracketed]` part for your own details.

## Made-up example

**You paste:** A Senior Software Engineer, GenAI role that mentions LLM evals and a hybrid schedule. Your background includes Python APIs and an internal RAG prototype.

**A strong question back:**

“How many days a week would I be in the office, and is that set or flexible?”

## Then do this

- [ ] Circle the 2 or 3 questions you actually care about
- [ ] Pick one real story for each example
- [ ] Say it your way on the call. No script.

# 04 · Stop rewriting the follow-up.

**For anyone after a conversation** · About 3 min

> **Use it when:** You just finished a kickoff, networking call or project chat.  
> **Have ready:** A few rough bullets. That is enough.

## Copy this

```text
Turn these rough notes into a short follow-up I can review and send.

Use three sections: Agreed, Still open, Next steps.
For each next step, include an owner and a deadline only if my notes say so. Otherwise write "to confirm".

Keep maybes separate from commitments. Do not add promises, decisions or dates. Plain, warm language. Under 150 words.

NOTES: [paste your bullets]
```

Swap every `[bracketed]` part for your own details.

## Made-up example

**You paste:** “Sam sends the AI Engineer brief Friday. Comp still undecided. Maybe ask Lee to join the next call.”

**A good draft keeps it straight:**

- **Agreed:** Sam, brief, Friday
- **Open:** compensation
- **Maybe:** Lee joins. Not “Lee is coming.”

- [ ] Check every name, date and promise
- [ ] Change one line so it sounds like you
- [ ] Hit send yourself, today

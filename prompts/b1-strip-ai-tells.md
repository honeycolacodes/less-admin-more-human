# B1 · Strip the AI tells.

**Bonus · for anything AI writes** · About 1 min

> **Use it when:** The facts are right but the draft reads like a template.  
> **Have ready:** The draft

## Watch for these tells

- Long dashes ( — )
- “I’d love to”
- “I hope this finds you well”
- “Delve,” “leverage,” “elevate”
- Lists of three, everywhere
- Every sentence the same length

## Copy this

```text
Edit this so it does not read like AI wrote it. Remove long dashes, filler openers, buzzwords and anything that sounds like a template. Mix short and long sentences. Keep every fact and do not add new ones.

TEXT: [paste the draft]
```

Swap every `[bracketed]` part for your own details.

## Made-up example

**You paste:** “I hope this finds you well! I’d love to connect and explore how we can leverage synergies.”

**Sounds like a person:**

“Hi Sam, good to meet you at the meetup. Want to grab 20 minutes next week to compare notes on hiring?”

- [ ] Check the facts survived
- [ ] Make sure nothing new was added
- [ ] Send it
